Our Story

Born from a curiosity for what coffee can truly be.

Founded in 2021 by a team of biochemists and champion baristas, The Coffee Lab began as a modest roasting workshop dedicated to decoding the flavor kinetics of coffee extraction.

We view roasting not just as a culinary craft, but as a discipline of thermodynamics and chemistry. By precisely tuning roasting temperatures across the drying, Maillard, and development phases, we reveal delicate floral top-notes, vibrant fruit acidity, and deep caramelized sweetness that industrial roasters burn away.
